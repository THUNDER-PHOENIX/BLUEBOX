Badminton
稲本 大樹

Taiki Inomata

Protagonist
Age16
Year1st
ClubBadminton
GoalNational
Earnest Hardworking Shy Determined

A first-year student with a burning passion for badminton, Taiki dedicates every morning to training in the gym. He falls deeply in love with Chinatsu Kano after watching her play basketball. When Chinatsu unexpectedly becomes his housemate, Taiki must navigate the impossible challenge of keeping his feelings secret while growing closer to her every day.

"I'll become someone worthy of standing beside her."

Chinatsu Kano

Main Heroine
Age16
Year1st
ClubBasketball
GoalNational
Cool Dedicated Warm Talented

An exceptionally talented basketball player admired throughout the school, Chinatsu carries a calm and collected aura. Beneath her composed exterior is a warm, caring person who pours everything into her sport. After a series of circumstances, she moves into Taiki's home — turning both their worlds upside down.

"I don't want to rely on others. But maybe… that's okay sometimes."

Taiki's younger sister who immediately falls in love with Chinatsu. Hina acts as the heart of the household — always bright, energetic, and somehow knowing exactly what everyone is feeling. She supports her brother's feelings from the shadows while treating Chinatsu like an older sister she always wanted.

"Chinatsu-san is the best! I want her to stay foreveeer!"

Taiki's best friend and badminton club teammate. Kyo is easygoing and laid-back but surprisingly perceptive when it comes to emotions and relationships. He's the first to notice Taiki's feelings for Chinatsu and becomes his biggest (if teasing) supporter. A reliable presence both on and off the court.

"Bro, just tell her already. I'm dying watching you hold it in."
